# Claims policy

## Required

- Say when findings or revenue are simulated.
- Cite the named public evidence that made the agency relevant.
- Describe AgentAmp as testing and preparing recommendations, not automatically changing stores.
- Include a simple opt-out on cold email.

## Forbidden without direct evidence

- Guaranteed revenue or conversion gains.
- Claims that Shopify OAuth is live for arbitrary stores.
- Claims that a simulated purchase is real revenue.
- Claims that AgentAmp will edit a merchant's live store in v1.
- Invented client names, portfolio work, metrics, quotes, or relationships.
